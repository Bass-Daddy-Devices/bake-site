BAKE 1.0.0

The first release of BAKE: an offline, node-based audio workstation for resampling, on macOS 26 or later with Apple Silicon.

Wire nodes into a patch, then bake it to a file. Nothing runs in real time, so every node can look at the whole sound, take as long as it needs, and use the GPU.

  • 75 nodes: filters, distortion and dynamics; time effects (delay, reverb, granular, beat repeat, stutter, step FX, stretch); spectral nodes (vocoder, spectral resynthesis, fractal filter, OTFT compressor); wavetables for Serum and Vital; routing; and modulation (LFOs, envelopes, followers, clocks, logic).
  • Batch processing: run a patch over a folder, slices of a long recording, or chunks caught at each trigger; sort, split, layer, average, scatter, conform to one tempo and key.
  • Modulate anything: almost every sound-shaping control takes a modulation signal.
  • Custom nodes: save a group of nodes as your own node.
  • Deterministic: the same patch and inputs always bake the same file, bit for bit.
  • Updates: BAKE checks for new versions (Settings › Updates). Every update is signed and notarized.

The documentation, with a page for every node, is in the wiki: https://github.com/Bass-Daddy-Devices/BAKE/wiki
